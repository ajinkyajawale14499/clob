LOB mid-price prediction: published anchors for our LightGBM build

Each chart is calibrated against one paper or one well-known benchmark. Use these as the 'what's realistic' reference when interpreting your own results.

1 - Prediction horizons used in the literature

Take-away: K = 100 events is the longest commonly-tested horizon. It is a safe choice; we have direct comparables.

2 - Class balance vs horizon and tick-class

Take-away: If you binarise sign(mid) on large-tick names, ~70-95% of rows are zero-move. Use a 1-tick deadband (3-class) or restrict to small/medium-tick stocks.

3 - Realistic AUC target band

Take-away: Target AUC ~0.58-0.62 at K=100 on raw LOBSTER. >0.70 = treat as leakage and audit.

4 - Walk-forward train/val/test split

Take-away: Walk-forward with sequential test days is the only credible split. No random shuffling.

5 - Confusion-matrix reality on raw LOBSTER

Take-away: Confusion matrices on real data show errors concentrate between Up and Down for small-tick stocks - the model knows there's motion but not the direction.

6 - LightGBM hyperparameter starting ranges

Take-away: Start LightGBM at num_leaves=64-128, learning_rate=0.03, min_data_in_leaf=500. Sweep with Optuna and use early stopping on validation log-loss.
